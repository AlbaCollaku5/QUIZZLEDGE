# 🎓 QuizZledge

**QuizZledge** is a fun and interactive quiz website built as a school project for a Web Development course by six passionate developers: A.C, S.B, CH.H, S.M, P.M, and E.P.

---

## 🌟 About the Project

**QuizZledge** offers a dynamic quiz-playing experience with a wide variety of categories to choose from, such as:

- Anime
- Animals
- Art
- Science
- Geography
- History  
- ...and many more!

Whether you're a trivia lover or just looking for some fun with friends, QuizZledge has something for everyone.

---

## 🔑 Features

- 🎮 **Play quizzes** from multiple categories
- 🏆 **Leaderboard system** to view rankings compared to other players
- 👥 **User authentication** (Login & Logout)
- ✍️ **Create your own quizzes** and share them with others
- 💬 **Invite friends, family, or loved ones** to enjoy personalized quiz experiences
